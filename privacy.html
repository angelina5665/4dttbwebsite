Technical information

Cookies and analytics

The current website source does not include a first-party analytics or advertising tracker. If measurement tools are added later, this notice should be updated before they are enabled.

Daily glossary selection

The Chinese number-symbolism glossary stores only the selected educational entry ID and its Malaysia calendar date in local storage on your browser. This keeps the daily reading consistent when you return. The stored selection is replaced on a later day's visit; this feature does not create a visitor identifier or transmit the selection to our server.

Different browsers and devices have separate selections, and different visitors may receive the same entry. Clearing this site's browser data removes the saved choice. If storage is unavailable, the reading stays only while that page remains open and may change on reload. The glossary can still be read without storage or JavaScript.

Dictionary searches, source filters and the graphic toggle run in your browser. The four-digit browsing code is derived from the selected cultural motif count and is not stored separately.

The separate Dictionary page downloads a static copy of the Da Ma Cai dictionary to your browser. Its number and name searches, category filters and result paging run locally in your browser; your typed query is not submitted as a search request, and the page does not save your searches. When a pictured Dream or Zodiac result is displayed, your browser requests its illustration directly from Da Ma Cai or its image host. The image URL identifies the displayed number or animal, so those hosts can infer which pictured entries were viewed and may receive standard request information, including your IP address. Image requests use a no-referrer policy.

The English, Chinese or Malay language choice is saved in this browser's local storage so the results, Dictionary and prize-history pages can remember it. The choice is not a visitor identifier or sent to a separate language service.

A small sponsored banner may appear once per browser tab session on the results, Dictionary or prize-history page. The site saves only a shown/not-shown flag in session storage to avoid repeating it during that session. The banner uses an image hosted on this site and does not open another window automatically. Its affiliate destination is visited only if you choose to click it.

On the results page, clicking the prominently labeled Dictionary link has a 25% chance of also opening the existing TTBet affiliate destination in a new tab. This is attempted at most once per browser tab session, even when the chance does not result in an ad. The Dictionary link continues to work normally. A browser may block the additional tab. The site stores only an attempted/not-attempted flag in session storage; the decision is made locally and is not sent to our server.

The prize-history page downloads a dated static draw archive and filters it in your browser. Entering a number on that page does not submit a search request. If you follow a number-specific history link from the Dictionary, the four digits appear in the destination URL and may be present in ordinary server request logs or browser history. The archive links to its original licensed dataset and separate official operator histories for Jackpot products.

External and affiliate links

Some links lead to independent third-party websites and may be affiliate links. Those websites control their own data practices, cookies, accounts, and transactions. Review their privacy information before providing data.
