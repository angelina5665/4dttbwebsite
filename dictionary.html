About these entries

This is a dated snapshot of Da Ma Cai’s public Numbers Dictionary, exported on 17 September 2026. It contains bilingual Dream Numbers plus the English and Chinese Zodiac and Festive pages. Dream and Zodiac result pictures load from Da Ma Cai’s published image URLs; Festive entries have no corresponding source pictures. Some source-language pages disagree on zodiac codes; the results identify whether a code appeared on the English page, Chinese page, or both. 4D VIP is not affiliated with Da Ma Cai.

Dictionary associations are entertainment, not a recommendation or a way to improve the chance of winning. Read the source’s disclaimer.
